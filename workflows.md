# AdvisoryOS Workflows

AdvisoryOS is being developed around a small number of high-value workflows rather than a large number of shallow agents.

## 1. Meeting Capture & Action Orchestrator

The system processes meeting information and extracts useful advisory context.

### Inputs

- Meeting notes
- Transcripts
- Emails
- Uploaded documents
- Existing project information

### Intelligence

The workflow can identify:

- Decisions
- Commitments
- Concerns
- New information
- Unresolved questions
- Stakeholder positions

### Actions

Extracted actions can include:

- Task
- Owner
- Deadline
- Dependency
- Priority

Confirmed conclusions can become retrievable project knowledge according to the knowledge governance rules.

---

## 2. Mandate Qualification

AdvisoryOS is designed to help evaluate potential advisory opportunities.

The workflow can consider factors such as:

- Strategic fit
- Potential conversion
- Expected fee
- Retainer potential
- Upside
- Effort
- Complexity
- Relationship value
- Geographic fit
- Ability to add value
- Execution burden
- Opportunity cost

The system is designed to support human decision-making rather than automatically committing to external actions.

---

## 3. Advisory Preparation Brief

AdvisoryOS can prepare a concise briefing for an upcoming meeting.

A preparation brief can bring together:

- Previous meeting information
- Open actions
- Recent correspondence
- Relevant documents
- Outstanding decisions
- Stakeholder positions
- Recent work products

The resulting brief can be structured around:

- What changed
- Important facts
- Open decisions
- Stakeholder positions
- Risks and issues
- Recommended position
- Questions to ask
- Actions to push

---

## 4. Document Review & Comparison

The Telegram interface is intended to support document uploads and analysis.

A user can provide a document and request operations such as:

- Review the document
- Compare it with another version
- Identify substantive changes
- Analyse relevant implications
- Produce a longer deliverable when required

---

## 5. Knowledge Retrieval

AdvisoryOS is designed to retrieve information from approved knowledge sources.

The intended retrieval hierarchy is:

1. Direct reference
2. Deterministic search
3. Semantic/QMD search
4. Agent reasoning

Source restrictions and traceability are important parts of the design.

---

## 6. Growth Engine

A later workflow focuses on turning market signals and insights into useful content and potential commercial opportunities.

The intended chain is:

Signal
→ Relevance
→ Point of view
→ Content
→ Channel adaptation
→ Relevant people/accounts
→ Human-approved engagement
→ Response
→ Funnel attribution

External publishing and communication require human approval.

---

## Development Status

These workflows represent the current development direction of AdvisoryOS.

Individual workflows may be incomplete or under active development. This repository should not be interpreted as claiming that every workflow described here is production-complete.
